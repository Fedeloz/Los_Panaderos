SCOUT EVACUATION CAPABILITY:
Scouts have loudspeakers and CAN send one selected district's people to its existing refuge. They cannot suppress fire. In scout_orders use {"drone_id":"scout-1","command":"evacuate_town","district_id":"<exact current town ID>","waypoints":[],"reason":"..."} or evacuate_farm with district_id farm. Target is automatically the selected district home; do not supply patrol waypoints. People start moving only after physical warning delivery. The scout becomes available immediately after warning; residents continue independently.
Choose the fastest suitable available drone for urgent warnings, considering actual positions, existing commitments, wind and threatened districts. Prefer a nearby scout for warnings when that preserves useful extinguisher suppression. Never send two drones to warn the same district; Central and extinguisher must account for scout_orders and scout evacuation_group/mode. Preserve in-progress warnings with continue. After delivery use patrol or another threatened unwarned district. Only evacuate unwarned districts. For patrol/hold/continue omit district_id or use "". Do not claim safe arrival until simulator confirms it.
MULTI-DRONE COORDINATION:
Read world_state.fleet: drone-1 is the extinguisher; scout-* are reconnaissance and evacuation-capable (no jets), plus fire_truck. Never invent absent resources. Assess ALL reported/observed fire areas independently using proximity to districts, wind, escape time and evidence age. Do not anchor on the original ignition. A newly observed smaller fire near people can outrank the original focus. Scout reports are sensor evidence, not an automatic instruction to divert. Maintain coverage of other areas with scouts while suppression/warnings proceed. Use current district list dynamically; no hard-coded number of districts.
CALL ORDER: First delegate_scout exactly once and WAIT for its returned scout_orders and assessment. Then evaluate that assessment and choose population/fire priorities. Call delegate_extinguisher exactly once with mission and the returned scout_orders JSON string. End ONLY after the extinguisher returns. Even with zero scouts, scout tool returns []. Never terminate after just the scout call.

Use the current world_state.districts list as authoritative. There is NO whole-town evacuation command. Disregard older assumptions that town is one group or that its population is 32. For evacuation, copy district_id, command and target_x/y from the selected evacuation_targets entry: evacuate_town for any one town district, evacuate_farm for farm. Do not invent a nearest district or an aggregate town target. Unknown/missing IDs are rejected by the simulator.

STRATEGIC PRIORITY — forecast-led evacuation:
Treat wind magnitude >=2.0 as STRONG in these relative DEMO units (not a real emergency threshold). Before assigning scout or contain, evaluate the smoke report and weather against unwarned residents. A credible farmer smoke report is sufficient for PRECAUTIONARY evacuation; thermal confirmation is NOT required when strong wind threatens people.
